Structured Learning

Beginner Pottery Course

Design patterns created for absolute beginners who want to explore and conquer the fundamentals of pottery through both handbuilding and wheel throwing techniques.

Course Structure

⏱️ 10 Sessions • 2 Hours Per Session

What You Will Learn

  • ✦ Basic handbuilding foundations
  • ✦ Clay preparation and wheel centering
  • ✦ Opening clay and pulling uniform walls
  • ✦ Forming cups, bowls, plates, and small pots
  • ✦ Trimming, turning, and refining foot rings
  • ✦ Introduction to studio glazing techniques
  • ✦ Firing cycle steps: tracking greenware to finished ceramic pieces
All premium raw clays, specialty tools, and fluid materials are completely provided. Studio aprons are washed and ready for your arrival. Your enrollment covers the complete firing and glazing cycles for up to 5 selected pieces.

Good to Know

Studio Guidelines & FAQ

When can I collect my finished pottery?

Handcrafted clay goes through a patient journey—requiring thorough air-drying followed by precise bisque and glaze kiln firings. Your finished earthenware will be ready for collection approximately 3 to 4 weeks after your session.

What should I wear to a workshop?

Don't worry about the mess—clay is completely water-soluble and washes out easily! We highly recommend wearing comfortable, casual clothes that you don't mind getting a little dusty. Studio aprons are provided.

What is your booking and cancellation policy?

To preserve the intimate environment of our studio, wheel allocations are strictly limited and reserved exclusively for your scheduled time slot. Sessions can be rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance.
