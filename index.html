Beginner Pottery Course

Designed for absolute beginners who want to explore and conquer the fundamentals of pottery through both handbuilding and wheel throwing techniques.

⏱️ 10 Sessions • 2 Hours Per Session

What You Will Learn

  • ✦ Basic handbuilding foundations
  • ✦ Clay preparation and centering
  • ✦ Opening clay and pulling uniform walls
  • ✦ Forming cups, bowls, and plates
  • ✦ Trimming and foot ring refinement
  • ✦ Studio glaze process overview
All materials and aprons provided. Tuition covers firing/glazing cycles for up to 5 selected pieces.

Good to Know

Studio Guidelines & FAQ

When can I collect my finished pottery?

Handcrafted clay requires air-drying followed by precise bisque and glaze kiln firings. Your finished pieces will be ready for collection approximately 3 to 4 weeks after your session.

What should I wear to a workshop?

Clay is completely water-soluble and washes out easily. We recommend wearing comfortable clothes that you don't mind getting dusty. Studio aprons are provided.

What is your booking and cancellation policy?

To preserve our intimate environment, wheel allocations are strictly reserved for your scheduled slot. Sessions can be rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance.
