Good to Know

Studio Guidelines & FAQ

When can I collect my finished pottery?

Handcrafted clay goes through a patient journey—requiring thorough air-drying followed by precise bisque and glaze kiln firings. Your finished earthenware will be ready for collection approximately 3 to 4 weeks after your session.

🎨 Want to add color? If you would like to personally paint your piece, we offer an intermediate session to apply underglazes right after the initial bisque firing, just before we apply the final protective glaze.

What should I wear to a workshop?

Don't worry about the mess—clay is completely water-soluble and washes out easily! We highly recommend wearing comfortable, casual clothes that you don't mind getting a little dusty. We will provide you with a studio apron to protect your attire while you create.

👋 Pro-tip for the wheel: For the best experience and grip on the clay, please keep long hair tied back, and we highly recommend trimming your nails short!

What is your booking and cancellation policy?

To preserve the intimate environment of our studio, wheel allocations are strictly limited and reserved exclusively for your scheduled time slot. Because of this personal setup, sessions can be rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance. Late cancellations or no-shows are non-refundable.
